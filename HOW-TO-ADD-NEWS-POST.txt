HOW TO ADD A NEW NEWS POST
==========================

There are two things to add each time: a card (the visual tile on the page)
and a post entry (the full content that opens when someone clicks Read More).


STEP 1 - Save your image
-------------------------
Drop the photo into:
  Documents > innovate-recruit-sandbox > site > images

Give it a simple filename with no spaces, like: jane-smith.png

The rendered image size on the card is 290x163 pixels.
Crop or resize your photo to those dimensions before saving for best results.


STEP 2 - Open news.html
------------------------
Go to:
  Documents > innovate-recruit-sandbox > site > news.html

Right-click it and open with Notepad (or VS Code).


STEP 3 - Add the card
----------------------
Find this line near the bottom of the existing cards:

      </div>
    </div>
  </section>

Just ABOVE that block, paste a new card using this template:

        <article class="post-card">
          <div class="post-card__img">
            <img src="/images/jane-smith.png" alt="Welcome Jane Smith" />
          </div>
          <div class="post-card__body">
            <p class="post-card__meta">2026 &middot; Leadership</p>
            <h2 class="post-card__title">Your post title here</h2>
            <p class="post-card__excerpt">One or two sentences that preview the post. Keep it short.</p>
            <button class="post-card__btn" onclick="openPost('jane')">Read More</button>
          </div>
        </article>

IMPORTANT: The word inside openPost('jane') is your post ID.
Pick anything short with no spaces. You will use the same word in Step 4.


STEP 4 - Add the post content
------------------------------
In the same news.html file, scroll down and find this line:

  var posts = {

Add a new entry BEFORE the closing }; using this template:

    jane: {
      img: '/images/jane-smith.png',
      meta: '2026 &middot; Leadership',
      title: 'Your post title here',
      content: '<p>First paragraph of the full post.</p><p>Second paragraph.</p>'
    },

IMPORTANT: The word before the colon (jane:) must match exactly what you
put in openPost('jane') in Step 3.


STEP 5 - Save and refresh
--------------------------
1. Save news.html
2. Double-click refresh.bat in the innovate-recruit-sandbox folder
3. Refresh localhost:8080/news.html in your browser


RULES TO REMEMBER
-----------------
- No spaces in the post ID or image filename
- Wrap all paragraph text in <p> and </p> inside the content string
- End every entry in the posts object with a comma (,) except the very last one
- To change the category label, edit the text after &middot; in the meta line
  Examples: Leadership, Office Opening, Company News, Agent Spotlight
