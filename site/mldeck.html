Competitive Compensation

Five Ways to Earn.

Most brokerages offer one income source. INNOVATE Market Center Leaders earn from five distinct streams simultaneously, and those streams compound as your market grows.

Income Stream 01

Commissions on Every Sale

Market Leaders receive 85/15 commission splits on their personal sales, plus a percentage of company dollar generated by every agent closing in your market center.

85% commission split on personal production
Income Stream 02

Market Center Override

You earn 15% of the net company dollar generated by every agent in your market center. This recurring income grows with every agent you add and every agent who produces.

15% of Net Market Company Dollar (12.75% of total CO$)
Income Stream 03

Revenue Share

INNOVATE’s five-tier revenue share program generates passive income from agents you personally sponsor into the company, and from their networks across five tiers.

5 tiers / 5-4-3-2-1% structure
Income Stream 04

Launch Coach Bonus

As the launch coach for new agents joining your market, you receive 25% off the top of each new agent’s first three closings as recognition for their early-stage development.

25% off the top of each new agent’s first 3 closings
Income Stream 05

Equity + Broker Fees

INNOVATE gives private equity shares so you share in company growth at every level. Once a physical location is established, broker admin fees are passed directly to you at $240 per transaction.

Private equity shares + $240/transaction with office space

Today, INNOVATE operates across 14 states with the same core belief it started with: a brokerage should do more than house agents. It should help them build something meaningful.
