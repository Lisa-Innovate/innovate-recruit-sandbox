The INNOVATE Story

Built Different. On Purpose.

INNOVATE didn’t start with a national expansion strategy or a corporate blueprint. It started with two agents and team leaders who believed the brokerage experience could be better.

At the time, the industry forced agents into trade-offs. Traditional brokerages offered local support, culture, and accountability, but often lacked the technology, scalability, and financial alignment modern agents were looking for. On the other side, newer cloud-based models offered flexibility, revenue share, and expansion opportunities, but many agents felt disconnected from leadership, training, and hands-on support.

Jeff and Darren saw those gaps firsthand while operating as a successful team inside a large franchise system. They believed agents deserved a brokerage built around helping them grow, not simply a system where agents paid fees in exchange for a logo and basic infrastructure.

So they started building differently. They focused on creating a company where technology actually helped agents produce more. Where training wasn’t theoretical. Where support was accessible. Where teams could scale intelligently. And where agents had the opportunity to participate financially in the growth they were helping create.

What started as a small independent brokerage in Myrtle Beach grew quickly because agents responded to something that felt different. The culture felt more connected. Leadership was accessible. The systems were practical. The environment was growth-minded without losing the personal relationships that matter in real estate.

Interestingly, expansion wasn’t the original goal. But agents in other markets started reaching out wanting to be part of what was being built. That forced leadership to think carefully about the future. Because growth can strengthen a company, but it can also weaken culture, dilute support, and create distance between leadership and agents if it’s done poorly.

That’s why INNOVATE expanded intentionally. The company doubled down on the principles that built the original brokerage:

  • Strong broker support
  • Practical training
  • Real accountability
  • High-level technology
  • A collaborative culture built around helping agents succeed

Leadership recognized something happening across the industry long before many others did: the future wasn’t going to belong entirely to traditional franchises or entirely to cloud brokerages. Agents needed a better combination of both. That realization became the foundation for INNOVATE’s Hybrid Growth Model.

Today, INNOVATE combines the culture, support, and accountability of strong local brokerages with the scalability, systems, technology, and long-term financial opportunities of modern platforms. And unlike many companies that expand primarily for corporate growth, INNOVATE built revenue share, equity opportunities, leadership pathways, and scalable business models so agents could benefit from the growth they were helping create.

At its core, the mission remains simple: build a brokerage that helps agents grow professionally, financially, and personally while creating a company designed for where the industry is heading next.
